> Somewhere between Prince Myshkin and 451 degrees, I keep a small room for attention:
> notes, tools, and questions that refuse to become slogans.

I work around agents, knowledge systems, and personal publishing.

These days I am mostly learning by building, reading other people's code, and
contributing where I can. I like software that makes thought more legible without
flattening it into a pitch.

```text
current coordinates
  agents as collaborators, not mascots
  retrieval, memory, and useful context
  personal websites as thinking rooms
  small tools that leave better traces
```

I am especially interested in the spaces where engineering starts to feel like
editing: choosing what to keep, what to name, what to connect, and what to leave
quiet.
